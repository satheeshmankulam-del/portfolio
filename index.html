com.satheesh.linked

linked

Built because of a personal tab-hoarding problem. Linked is a visual bookmark manager — it fetches OG metadata so your saved links look like what they actually are, not just raw URLs. Organised into Vaults: Typography, Motion, Case Studies, Inspiration, and more.

Visual bookmarks — OG image, title & domain (Microlink API) Vaults for organised collections with colours & icons Tags, search history, dark & light theme PIN lock, trash, haptic feedback
